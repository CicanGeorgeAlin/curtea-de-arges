# PASS 102 — MNAR C44 / C81

Date: 2026-09-22

## Focus

Object identity and museum-inventory control for the two tile fragments associated with the 1920 Curtea Domnească excavation.

## Controlled results

- Rădulescu 2000 records the MNAR pair as **4119/C44** and **4130/C81**.
- The same 2000 discussion explicitly describes the fragments as **nesmălțuite (unglazed)**.
- Rădulescu 2007 records the pair as **4119/C44** and **10073/C81**.
- The 2007 discussion describes the pair as green/light-green glazed.
- The 2007 publication cites Rădulescu 2000 for chronology, iconography, analogies, technique and dimensions.
- C44 is stable at 4119 across the two publications.
- C81 remains continuously designated C81, but its numerical inventory identifier differs: **4130 vs 10073**.
- No independent museum/object record establishing **4130 = 10073** was located in this pass.
- No evidence was found that permits a choice among inventory renumbering, publication error, conservation/restoration, different object state, different fragment, or catalogue transcription as the explanation for the discrepancy.
- The 1923 BCMI table of materials identifies figs. 78–79 as “Teracote găsite în Casa Domnească” and fig. 80 as “Teracotă găsită în Casa Domnească.”
- Direct plate-level visual assignment of figs. 78–80 to C44/C81 remains open.

## Interpretation boundary

The evidence supports a **strong bibliographic continuity** between the 2000 and 2007 C44/C81 discussions, but does not yet prove a physical-object equivalence for numerical identifiers 4130 and 10073.

The archive therefore preserves:

**C44 = 4119 — stable**

**C81 = 4130 (2000) / 10073 (2007) — unresolved**

**2000 = unglazed / 2007 = glazed — unresolved material-description contradiction**

## Next target

Primary visual control of Drăghiceanu 1923 figs. 78–80, followed by any museum catalogue/conservation/accession evidence that can establish the history of C81.
