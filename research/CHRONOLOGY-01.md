Project: **Curtea de Argeș — Arhiva Vie**  
Creator and curator: **Cican George Alin**

This is an evidence table, not yet the final narrative chronology. Dates remain at the precision supported by the cited source.

## Chronology gaps requiring research

- Earliest securely located archaeological evidence within the modern municipal territory.
- Prehistoric settlement sites and exact provenance of museum objects.
- Ancient-period settlement patterns and road/river relationships.
- Early medieval evidence before the documented princely centre.
- Documentary attestations and the exact evolution of the medieval political centre.
- The chronology and spatial development of the two medieval court complexes.
- The chronology of churches, cemeteries and religious communities.
- The development of streets, markets, crafts and trade.
- Administrative changes and municipal institutions.
- Education, health, printing, associations and everyday urban life.
- Railway construction, operation and social/economic effects.
- World War I and World War II.
- Communist-era urban transformation and heritage policy.
- Post-1989 urban, demographic and economic change.
- Current heritage condition and conservation status as of 2026.

## Methodological warning

The chronology must never be made artificially smooth. Where evidence is absent, the timeline should say that evidence is absent. Where historians disagree, the disagreement itself becomes part of the archive.
