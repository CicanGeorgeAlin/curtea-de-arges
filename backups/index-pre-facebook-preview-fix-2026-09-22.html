01 · Mission

A city. A history. A memory.

This is the beginning of a historical, archaeological, architectural and cultural archive dedicated to Curtea de Argeș. It is not simply a tourism list. It is a research infrastructure that grows through verification, documentation and continuous revision.

Time

A chronology from the earliest accessible evidence to contemporary Curtea de Argeș.

Place

Every significant site, monument and place can be connected to its geographical position.

People

Rulers, clergy, artists, craftspeople, researchers and the people of the city, wherever the sources allow.

Evidence

Important claims retain their source, source type and differences between interpretations.

04 · Research standard

We do not invent history.

When sources differ, the difference remains visible. When a fact cannot be established, we mark it as unknown or disputed. Documents, academic interpretation, local tradition and oral memory are not mixed together.

Primary Sources

Documents, inscriptions, maps, registers, artefacts and testimony contemporary with the events.

Institutions

Heritage bodies, museums, archives, libraries, administration and official databases.

Research

Archaeological, historical and academic studies, with verifiable bibliography and provenance.

Memory

Local photographs and stories preserved as sources of memory and identity, distinct from verified historical claims.
