04A · Wider ceramic context

Unglazed and glazed both occur in the corpus

A 1967 specialist discussion preserves Drăghiceanu's broader distinction between older unglazed terracottas and later glazed examples. This is useful context, but it does not identify the individual MNAR C44/C81 fragments.

Research control: contextual evidence is not object-level evidence. The 2000/2007 C44/C81 contradiction remains open.
